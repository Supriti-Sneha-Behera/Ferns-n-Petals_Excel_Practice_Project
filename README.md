# Ferns-n-Petals_Excel_Practice_Project

A comprehensive sales performance, customer behavior, and product analysis project for Ferns and Petals (FNP)—specializing in occasion-based gifting (Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Valentine's Day, Birthdays, and Anniversaries). This repository contains raw datasets, data cleaning workflows, analytical findings, and an interactive executive dashboard built to optimize sales strategy and customer satisfaction.   

# Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel

# Project Archietecture

* fernsandpetals_orders.csv - Transaction records dataset
* fernsandpetals_customers.csv - Customer demographic details
* fernsandpetals_products.csv - Product catalog details
* FernsNPetals_Excel_File.xlsx - Workbook containing models and sheets
* Sales_Analysis_Dashboard_Picture.png - Dashboard visual snapshot
* Questions.pdf - Project scope & business questions

# Key Problem Insights

The project addresses ten core business metrics outlined in the problem statement:   
* Total Revenue: Evaluates overall monetary intake across transactions.   
* Average Order & Delivery Time: Measures logistical turnaround and fulfillment efficiency.   
* Monthly Sales Performance: Analyzes revenue fluctuations across months.   
* Top Products by Revenue: Identifies high-performing revenue generators.   
* Customer Spending Analysis: Computes average customer spend patterns.   
* Top 5 Product Performance: Tracks specific revenue trajectories of leading SKUs.   
* Top 10 Cities by Orders: Locates key geographic hubs driving volume.   
* Order Quantity vs. Delivery Time: Assesses whether bulk orders strain logistics.   
* Revenue by Occasion: Compares income contribution across cultural and personal events.   
* Product Popularity by Occasion: Maps specific product preferences to festivals/occasions.   

# Key Findings

* Occasion Concentration: Diwali drives the vast majority of revenue, highlighting a strong seasonal spike that requires robust inventory planning ahead of Q4.
* Category Drivers: Sweets and Colors dominate category-wise sales, aligning tightly with festival gifting trends.
* Geographical Hubs: Cities like Bidhannagar and Maheshtala lead order frequency volume, pointing to strong tier-2 and tier-3 market engagement.
* Fulfillment Windows: Average delivery turnaround hovers around 5 days, maintaining steady fulfillment across varied order sizes.
